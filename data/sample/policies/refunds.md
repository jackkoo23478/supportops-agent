# Refund Policy

## refunds.processing

For an approved return, the refund is issued to the original payment
method after the returned item has been received and inspected.
Refund processing takes 5 to 10 business days after inspection.

## refunds.approval

Refunds must satisfy the applicable return policy.
Eligible refunds of USD 50.00 or less may be approved automatically.
Refunds above USD 50.00 require human approval before execution.

## refunds.shipping_fees

Original shipping fees are not refundable unless the wrong item
was shipped or the item was damaged on arrival.
These exceptions require manual review.
