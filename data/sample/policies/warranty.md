# Warranty Policy

## warranty.coverage

Electronic products carry a 12-month limited warranty from
the delivery date for manufacturing defects.

## warranty.exclusions

Accidental damage, misuse, and normal wear and tear are not covered.

## warranty.claims

Warranty claims require proof of purchase and a description of
the defect. A support ticket must be created for manual review.
Warranty coverage does not automatically authorize a refund.
